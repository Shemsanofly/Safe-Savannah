Camera or photo

Park cameras and ranger uploads send images of animals near lodges, camps, trails and roads.

Tourist SOS

One tap in the app, a USSD menu on any basic phone, or a call. Medical, wildlife, vehicle or personal safety.

Guests and tour groups

Guests check in at their lodge by QR, check out on game drives and walks, and ask for a night escort. A tour group is in the park once its guide is verified at the gate.

Admin

Draws safety zones; manages staff, lodges and tour operators; reads the tourism report: guests accounted for, response times.

Rangers

See it on the live map, press I'm responding, call the guide of any tour group nearby, coordinate by radio, then Resolve.

Lodges and tour operators

The front desk runs an "Are you safe?" roll call of every guest and sends escorts. Tour operators see alerts near their groups.

Tourists

A warning with what to do, I'm safe in one tap, then the all clear. SOS tracker: Received → Ranger responding → Resolved. Plus Today in the park (delayed, no places).
